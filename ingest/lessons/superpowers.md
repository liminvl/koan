The category leader's SDLC pack (form-matching technique + hook counter-evidence + a fourth convergence).

**Adopted (2026-07-11 triage)**
- **Match the form to the failure — a tested taxonomy for authoring behavior-shaping rules.** writing-skills classifies the *baseline failure* before picking the wording form: discipline violation (knows the rule, skips under pressure) → prohibition + rationalization table; wrong-shaped output → positive recipe stating what the output IS; omitted element → a REQUIRED structural slot; condition-dependent behavior → a conditional on an observable predicate. Backed by wording micro-tests: on a shaping problem the prohibition arm produced MORE of the unwanted content than the no-guidance control (fully separated distributions), and appending one nuance clause ("don't X unless it matters") degraded a winning recipe from consistent to noisy. Directly applicable to koan's own loop: when a delta probe fails to flip (D-013) or a core rule doesn't land on a model (D-018/D-023), the fix may be the *form*, not more words — koan has no guidance saying so. Free channel, zero shipped tokens. · superpowers skills/writing-skills "Match the Form to the Failure" · _adopted_ → "Form follows failure" section in `ingest/distill.md`, right after the fit-test.
- **Variance as a wording metric.** Their micro-test rule: when guidance lands, fresh-context reps converge on the same output shape; five different interpretations across five reps means the wording isn't binding — tighten the form before adding words. koan's behavior probes grade pass/fail per run (D-013/D-022) but never look at convergence *across* replicates as a signal; at promotion time (2-of-3, D-022) the shape-variance of the two passing runs is free information already sitting in the preserved answers. · superpowers skills/writing-skills micro-test rules · _adopted_ → one line in `benchmarks/README.md` Stage 1 (read D-022 replicates for convergence, not just the verdict; scattered interpretations = fix the form).
- **Workflow summaries in descriptions make agents skip the skill body.** Their tested SDO finding: a description that summarizes the workflow becomes a shortcut — an agent did ONE review when the description said "code review between tasks" though the body's flowchart showed two; trigger-conditions-only descriptions fixed it. koan's descriptions DO summarize workflow (koan-wrap's names updating HANDOFF, appending decisions, archiving, pruning — enough for an agent to act on without ever loading the routing table or budgets). · superpowers skills/writing-skills SDO section · _adopted_ → four descriptions reworded, trigger phrases kept, gated by `benchmarks/triggers.mjs` (12/12 rank-1 on first run, no prompt loosened): **koan-wrap** lost its full mechanism recipe (updates/appends/archives/prunes — the worst offender; body-only rules like the routing table and checkpoint semantics are no longer summarized where an agent could act on them without invoking); **koan-init** lost the seeds/backfills mechanism (whose description-level summary omitted the D-020 greenfield interview); **koan-readback** lost "read the project's memory files and" (which omitted the D-019 git cross-check — the exact step the description-shortcut would skip); **koan** core lost the ladder summary in the BUILD LESS parenthetical (compressed rules minus their "when NOT to be lazy" carve-outs; "yagni"/"simplest solution" stay as say-triggers). **koan-lint and koan-debt deliberately unchanged**: their what-it-checks/what-it-harvests enumerations ARE the pinned trigger vocabulary ("duplicate IDs", "dangling citations", "harvest", "ledger"), and neither has body-only safety steps a description-shortcut could skip.
